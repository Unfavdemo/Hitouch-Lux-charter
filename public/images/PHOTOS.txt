HiTouch image drop zones (Next.js serves files under /public at the site root).

Prefer WebP for site imagery (heroes max ~1920px, galleries ~1600px).
Keep a JPEG/PNG only when a platform requires it (e.g. OG: hitouch-logo-og.jpg).

FLEET — one folder per vehicle slug (matches content/fleet.js):
  /images/fleet/<slug>/
  Suggested files per folder:
    hero.webp
    gallery-1.webp, gallery-2.webp, …

MARKETING:
  /images/marketing/hero/
  /images/marketing/about/
  /images/marketing/services/
  /images/marketing/experiences/

BRAND:
  /images/hitouch-logo.webp          — site chrome
  /images/hitouch-logo-og.jpg        — Open Graph / social

After you add files, set paths in content/media.js `owned` and content/fleet.js.
